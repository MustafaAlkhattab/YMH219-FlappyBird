Exactly three wide landscape illustrations stacked vertically, equal heights, edge-to-edge with no separator lines, no borders, no words, no UI, no obstacles or characters. Overall portrait 2:3 canvas, each horizontal scene approx 2:1. Cohesive hand-painted animation background polish, muted palette and fine grain, clear open flying space for foreground game elements. TOP third: a turquoise Euphrates river canyon and misty amber cliffs with distant hills, early dawn, the horizon is slightly below center of that tile, delicate sun streaks. MIDDLE third: apricot and crimson sunset among high sandstone ridges and a faraway architectural ruin silhouette, arid valleys, broad warm apricot sky. BOTTOM third: luminous cool indigo moonlit plateau with muted violet rock ridges and faint stars and aurora, cold midnight. Composition for a side-scrolling flight game, background only, atmospheric perspective, distant silhouettes largely in lower half of each tile, no bright detail behind the central flight path, each tile complete and self-contained, absolutely no typography.
